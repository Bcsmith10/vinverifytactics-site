Before we arrive

Get ready to move fast

Every vehicle is checked against its paperwork, so allow at least 10 minutes per vehicle, and more if documents need sorting out. A little preparation keeps the visit short:

  • Park the vehicles where we can walk around each one and open the doors.
  • Have the ownership document for each vehicle ready: title, registration card, or MSO/MCO.
  • Make sure the VIN plate and the door-jamb label on each vehicle are visible and not covered or blocked.
  • Have keys available, so we can open doors and read the odometer.
  • Prepare a list of the vehicles with their VINs, if you have one.
  • Have one person on site who can answer questions and move vehicles.

Limits

What we can't verify

California requires some vehicles to be inspected directly by the CHP or DMV instead of a private verifier. These include revived salvage or junk vehicles, specially constructed vehicles (home-built, or kit and replica cars assembled for private use), incomplete vehicles, vehicles with a missing, damaged or altered VIN plate or federal safety label, ownership papers from a foreign country, and some motorcycles. The booking form flags these automatically.

Official rules: the DMV's Vehicle Verifications section of the Vehicle Industry Registration Procedures Manual. Confirm eligibility with us before booking, since only the DMV decides whether a form is accepted.
