California requires certain vehicles to be inspected directly by the CHP or DMV instead of a private verifier. Before booking, check whether any of the following apply — the form below will flag it automatically. You'll also need the vehicle's title, registration card, or MSO/MCO on hand.

  • Revived salvage or revived junk vehicle
  • Custom-built, kit-built, or replica vehicle
  • Incomplete or unassembled vehicle
  • VIN plate missing, damaged, illegible, or altered
  • Federal safety label (door-jamb sticker) missing, damaged, or altered
  • Ownership paperwork from a foreign country
  • Motorcycle with an engine change, or a used motorcycle last registered in California whose record no longer exists
  • 1981 or newer vehicle with a VIN shorter than 17 characters
